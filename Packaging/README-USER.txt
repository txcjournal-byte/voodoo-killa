TrapWin Timer
Halftime, tape and time FX for melodies.  https://trapwin.com

INSTALLED FILES
  Windows:  VST3        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\TrapWin Timer.vst3
            Standalone  C:\Program Files\TrapWin\TrapWin Timer\TrapWin Timer.exe
  macOS:    VST3        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/TrapWin Timer.vst3
            AU          /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/TrapWin Timer.component
            Standalone  /Applications/TrapWin Timer.app

FL STUDIO
  Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins, then add "TrapWin Timer" to a mixer insert
  on your melody channel. Click a category, click a card - done.

ABLETON LIVE
  Enable "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (Preferences > Plug-ins), rescan,
  then drag TrapWin Timer onto your melody track.

QUICK START
  Card click = preset A, Shift/right-click = preset B, then move MORPH or the KILL PAD.
  HALFTIME ENGINE: speed, loop length, Tape / Key / Hold, reverse, smooth, brake, fade.
  KILL = random preset (lock = current category), right-click KILL = mutate.
  Drag the bottom-right corner of the window to resize it.

User presets: Documents/TrapWin/TrapWin Timer/Presets
