`Resources/Photos/<file>` (file name in each heading). Missing photos fall back to `placeholder.jpg`.

Shared style base:

> Black and white flash photograph at night, heavy film grain, underground trap mixtape aesthetic, harsh flash, deep shadows, square 1:1, no text, no people's faces. Subject: <motif>
